General Rental Terms.

Terms applicable to the short-term rental of the Delfosse Properties apartments in Nice.

1. Purpose of the contract

These General Terms (hereinafter the "GRT") govern the short-term rental of the furnished apartments offered by Maxime Delfosse under the Delfosse Properties brand, located in Nice (06000), Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, France.

Any booking made directly on this site implies full and unreserved acceptance of these GRT. Bookings made through a third-party platform are governed by that platform's own terms.

2. Booking and payment

Bookings are made directly on delfosseproperties.com, via the site's secure booking module. Payment for the stay is collected by Delfosse Properties at the time of booking, by bank card, through the approved payment provider Stripe. Payment is secure; no bank-card data is stored by Delfosse Properties. The booking is confirmed only after payment has actually been received.

Prices are shown in euros (€) and include ordinary charges (water, electricity, internet). The summary shown before payment details the price of the nights, the cleaning fee and the tourist tax of the Métropole Nice Côte d'Azur, collected by Delfosse Properties on behalf of the local authority.

3. Security deposit

A card hold (pre-authorisation) of €300 for stays of fewer than 7 nights, or €600 for stays of 7 nights or more, may be placed on the guest's card shortly before arrival, as a security deposit. This hold is not charged: it is simply a temporary block, released after departure provided there is no damage and the house rules are respected. In the event of damage or breach, all or part of this amount may be charged, on presentation of supporting evidence, up to the pre-authorised amount. The Guest remains liable for any loss exceeding this amount.

4. Arrival and departure

Check-in: from 4:00 pm.
Check-out: before 11:00 am.

Late arrival or late departure is possible on request and subject to availability. The precise key-handover arrangements are communicated by email after the booking is confirmed. Depending on the apartment, keys are handed over via a secure lockbox or a concierge partner (KeyNest).

5. Guest capacity

Each apartment has a maximum capacity shown on its listing (2, 4 or 6 guests). The number of guests declared at booking may not be exceeded. Any undeclared excess may lead to immediate termination of the contract without refund.

6. House rules

  • Pets: not allowed, unless agreed in writing beforehand.
  • Smoking: smoking inside the apartments is strictly prohibited. In the event of a breach, cleaning and deodorising costs (indicatively ~€200) are charged at actual cost, on supporting evidence.
  • Noise: guests undertake to respect the peace of the neighbourhood, particularly between 10:00 pm and 8:00 am.
  • Parties / events: strictly prohibited. The capacity concerns overnight guests, not the hosting of additional visitors.
  • Subletting: strictly prohibited.

7. Cancellation and modification

Cancellation by the Guest: the cancellation policy is as follows: free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival (full refund of the nights); between 14 and 7 days before arrival, a 50 % refund of the nights; less than 7 days before arrival, the nights are non-refundable. The cleaning fee and the tourist tax are refunded in full in all cases. In the event of early departure by the Guest, unused nights are not refunded. Any cancellation or modification request is made by email to Delfosse Properties.

Cancellation by the Host: if Delfosse Properties cancels a direct booking of its own doing, the Guest is refunded in full for all sums paid, without prejudice to their right to compensation.

In the event of force majeure (unforeseeable event, natural disaster, armed conflict, etc.), a refund of the sums corresponding to the unused nights, or a postponement of the stay, will be offered.

In all circumstances, the Guest may contact Delfosse Properties to seek, by mutual agreement, an amicable solution (postponement of the stay, partial refund or goodwill gesture).

8. Guest obligations

  • Use the rented property reasonably, in accordance with its purpose as holiday accommodation.
  • Keep the property in good condition during the stay and report any damage to Maxime Delfosse as soon as possible.
  • Comply with the building's co-ownership rules (quiet, cleanliness of common areas, waste management).
  • Return the property in the condition in which it was handed over, following the end-of-stay instructions (clean dishes, emptied bins, keys returned as agreed).

9. Host obligations

Maxime Delfosse undertakes to:

  • Provide a clean apartment in good working and maintenance condition.
  • Supply the equipment advertised on the apartment's listing (Wi-Fi, air conditioning, bed linen, etc.).
  • Ensure a direct contact available 7 days a week during the stay for any question or emergency.
  • Guarantee peaceful enjoyment of the accommodation.

10. Insurance

The guest must be covered by "holiday" or "civil liability" insurance covering any damage caused to the rented accommodation. This cover is generally included in standard home-insurance policies.

11. Personal data

The personal data collected at booking is used exclusively to manage the stay (communication, inventory, invoicing, declaration to the authorities where required). For more details, see our Privacy policy.

12. Disputes and mediation

In the event of a dispute, an amicable solution will always be sought. Failing that, the Guest may refer the matter free of charge to the competent consumer mediator, in accordance with Articles L611-1 et seq. of the French Consumer Code.

These GRT are governed by French law. Any dispute falls within the jurisdiction of the French courts.

13. Changes to the terms

Maxime Delfosse reserves the right to amend these GRT at any time. The applicable version is the one in force at the date of booking.
