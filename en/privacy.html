Privacy policy.

How we collect, use and protect your personal data, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data controller

The controller of the data collected on delfosseproperties.com is:

Maxime Delfosse — Sole trader (non-professional furnished-rental landlord)
SIRET 893 745 851 00020 — 5 rue Barla, 06300 Nice, France
Contact: delfosseproperties@gmail.com

2. Data collected

The site collects no personal data passively (no advertising tracking, no profiling). The data collected is:

  • Language preference (FR/EN) — stored locally in your browser, never sent to the server.
  • Username and password for the guest area, only for guests who have already booked.
  • Booking data (first name, last name, email, phone, stay dates) — kept securely to manage your stay. We store no bank-card data.
  • Guest form (surname, first name, date and place of birth, nationality and home address of each occupant, and where applicable a copy of an identity document) — collected to meet the legal obligation to keep an individual police record for foreign travellers (art. R814-1 et seq. of the CESEDA). The identity-document copy is stored securely, with restricted access, and deleted automatically after your departure; the form data is kept for six months.

For a booking on this site, your details are used solely to manage your stay and to send your confirmation. Payment is processed by Stripe: no bank-card data is kept by Delfosse Properties (see Stripe's privacy policy).

3. Purposes of processing

The data collected is used exclusively to:

  • Manage your stay (before, during, after).
  • Communicate with you (confirmation, arrival instructions, assistance).
  • Comply with legal obligations (registration of tourist accommodation, tourist tax, individual police record for foreign travellers).
  • Improve the site and the user experience (anonymous statistics, where applicable).

4. Legal basis

Processing is based on:

  • Performance of the rental contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR) for managing the stay.
  • Legal obligation (art. 6.1.c GDPR) for registration with the town hall, the tourist tax and the individual police record for foreign travellers (art. R814-1 et seq. of the CESEDA).
  • Legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f GDPR) for the security of the guest area.

5. Retention period

Data is kept only for as long as necessary for its purpose:

  • Booking data: 3 years after the end of the stay (civil limitation period).
  • Guest form: 6 months (art. R814-2 of the CESEDA), then deleted.
  • Identity-document copy: deleted automatically at the end of the stay.
  • Accounting data: 10 years (legal obligation).
  • Guest area: until you request deletion.
  • Browser preferences: until you clear them.

6. Data recipients

Your data is never sold or transferred to third parties. It may be shared with:

  • Stripe (payment), Supabase (secure hosting of bookings) and Resend (email sending), strictly within the limits necessary.
  • Logistics partners (housekeeper, KeyNest concierge where applicable), only for the information needed for the service.
  • Authorities, on judicial request or legal obligation only.
  • Vercel (host of the site and of the serverless functions that process bookings).

Transfers outside the European Union. Some technical providers (notably Stripe for payment, Resend for emails and Vercel for hosting) may process data in the United States; hosting of the bookings database (Supabase) takes place in the region configured for the project. Where a transfer outside the EU occurs, it is governed by appropriate safeguards under Articles 44 et seq. of the GDPR (European Commission standard contractual clauses and/or participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework). Displaying the site also loads third-party resources (Google Fonts, flagcdn flag images, YouTube ambient video) that may receive your IP address.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access to your data.
  • Right to rectification if it is inaccurate.
  • Right to erasure (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restriction of processing.
  • Right to data portability.
  • Right to object to processing.
  • Right to withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact: delfosseproperties@gmail.com. A reply will be provided within one month.

If you have a complaint, you may also refer the matter to the CNIL (the French data protection authority): www.cnil.fr.

8. Cookies and trackers

The site uses a minimal number of technical cookies necessary for its operation:

  • dp_lang: remembers your language preference (FR/EN). Stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • dp_video_paused: remembers whether you paused the ambient video. localStorage.
  • Guest session: to keep you signed in to the private area during your stay.

No advertising cookies, no third-party tracking cookies (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.) are placed without your explicit consent. The ambient video is served by YouTube; you can pause it at any time via the dedicated control.

9. Security

The site is served entirely over HTTPS (TLS encryption), with strict security headers (X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy). The guest area is password-protected.

10. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be amended to reflect changes in regulations or in the site's services. The applicable version is the one in force at the time of your visit. Last updated: 25 November 2026.
